Privacy Policy – DESERT KNIGHTS

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Publisher: EG ONE GAMES · Contact: egonegames@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how EG ONE GAMES (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in the mobile game DESERT KNIGHTS (“the Game”). The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. This policy applies to the current Android version of the Game distributed through Google Play and the features described below. EG ONE GAMES does not operate its own game servers and does not receive your name, email address or payment details through the Game.

1. Gameplay and data stored on your device

You can play the single-player mode without creating an account. Local gameplay data, such as game progress, unlocked riders, skins and stages, upgrades, in-game coins and gems, achievement progress, settings and race results, is stored on your device. The Game also creates a random installation identifier on your device, used only to combine your progress correctly when it is restored from the cloud (see section 3). Local Wi-Fi multiplayer uses a local network connection between participating devices.

2. Google Play Games Services: sign-in and achievements

On Android, the Game uses Google Play Games Services, provided by Google. If your device has a Google Play Games profile, the Game signs you in to Google Play Games automatically, and you can also sign in from the main menu. Signing in is optional; the Game can be played without it.

  • The Game shows your Google Play Games display name on the main menu. The Game does not send this name to EG ONE GAMES.
  • The Game reports unlocked achievements and achievement progress to Google Play Games, where they appear in your Google Play Games profile.
  • Google processes your Play Games player identity (for example, gamer name and avatar), achievement data and related diagnostics under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its game data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

3. Cloud save (Google Play Games Saved Games)

When you are signed in to Google Play Games, the Game saves a copy of your progress in Google Play Games Saved Games, which Google stores with your Play Games profile. This lets you restore your progress after reinstalling the Game or on another Android device signed in to the same Play Games profile.

  • The cloud copy contains game progress only: coins, gems and the records of how they were earned, upgrades, unlocked riders, skins and stages, story progress, achievement progress, the random installation identifier, and records of completed purchases (a one-way hashed reference to each purchase, not your payment details).
  • Synchronization happens in the background, for example when you return to the main menu. The Game keeps working offline and synchronizes again when a connection is available.
  • Only the Game, while you are signed in to your own Play Games profile, can read or write this saved game. EG ONE GAMES has no server copy of it.
  • You can delete the saved game data together with your Play Games data through Google Play Games or your Google account settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android version of the Game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC and its affiliates):

  • Interstitial ads: full-screen ads that may appear between some races. They are never shown during a race.
  • Rewarded ads: optional ads that you choose to watch from the main menu in exchange for 2 gems, up to twice per day.

To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the Game collects and shares with Google information such as: your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), ad interactions (for example, ad views, taps and completed rewarded ads), and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google’s policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. EG ONE GAMES does not receive your advertising ID or personal information from these ads.

The Game is not configured to show ads directed at children. The Game does not show ads during races.

5. Your advertising choices and consent

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the Game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (a consent management platform certified for the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse consent and still play the Game; you may then see non-personalized or limited ads. Where Google requires it, you can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings › Privacy › AD PRIVACY OPTIONS.
  • All regions: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on the device).

6. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Android version offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing: gem packs (100, 600 and 1,000 gems) and a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Remove Ads removes the interstitial ads between races; the optional rewarded ads remain available.

  • Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The Game never sees or stores your card number or other payment details.
  • After a purchase, Google Play gives the Game a purchase confirmation (such as the product ID, an order or purchase token and a digital signature). The Game uses it on your device to verify the purchase, grant the item once and acknowledge or complete the purchase with Google Play. A hashed reference to the purchase is kept in your saved progress so that it is never granted twice.
  • Restoring purchases: Remove Ads is linked to the Google account used for the purchase. The Game checks your Google Play purchases automatically and when you tap RESTORE PURCHASES, for example after reinstalling. Gems that were already granted are restored through cloud save (section 3).
  • Refunds and purchase history are handled by Google Play under Google’s terms.

7. Online multiplayer and Epic Online Services

If you choose an online multiplayer feature, the Game uses Epic Online Services (EOS) to create or join online sessions. The online sign-in flow is initiated when you choose an online action, such as Quick Play, Create Room, Join or Retry. Merely opening the online menu does not, by itself, start the EOS sign-in flow.

To provide online multiplayer, the Game and EOS may process information such as:

  • Online service identifiers: an EOS Device ID used for the device-based sign-in flow and an EOS Product User ID (PUID) associated with the online player.
  • Session information: lobby or room membership, session identifiers, player status, race state and information needed to create, find, join and operate a multiplayer session.
  • Connection information: network and connection data, which may include IP address information, needed to establish and maintain online connections.
  • Gameplay communications: predefined in-game emoji and reactions selected by a player for communication during multiplayer gameplay.
  • Technical information: information required to operate the online service and troubleshoot connection or session problems.

The Game does not provide free-form text chat, voice chat, or player-to-player photo or file uploads. Online multiplayer is optional; you can use the single-player mode without starting the EOS online sign-in flow.

8. How we use information

Information handled by the Game is used to:

  • provide gameplay and keep your progress on your device and, when you are signed in, in Google Play Games Saved Games;
  • sign you in to Google Play Games and record achievements;
  • verify and deliver in-app purchases and restore Remove Ads;
  • show interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob and grant rewards for completed rewarded ads;
  • authenticate the player with the online multiplayer service, create, find, join and manage rooms and race sessions, and deliver predefined in-game reactions; and
  • maintain the security, reliability and proper operation of the Game and its online features.

The Game does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service operated by EG ONE GAMES. Google’s SDKs collect diagnostic and usage information for their own services as described in sections 2 to 6.

Information is transmitted to and processed by the following providers only as needed for the features you use: Google (Google Play Games Services, Saved Games, Google Play Billing and Google AdMob, including the advertising partners Google uses where you have consented) and Epic Games (Epic Online Services, for online multiplayer). Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy: the Google Privacy Policy and the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information. These statements do not describe or limit any processing Google or Epic performs under its own terms.

10. Data retention and deletion

Local game progress is stored on your device. You may remove local data through your device’s app-storage settings or by uninstalling the Game; the exact result depends on your device and operating system. Uninstalling also removes the consent choice stored on the device by Google’s User Messaging Platform.

Your cloud save and achievements are kept with your Google Play Games profile until you delete them through Google Play Games or your Google account. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its policies. Online service identifiers and session-related records may be processed or retained by Epic in accordance with its service operation, retention practices and privacy policy. We do not control Google’s or Epic’s independent retention systems and cannot promise that uninstalling the Game will delete information already processed by them.

For a privacy question or a request concerning information handled by EG ONE GAMES, contact us at egonegames@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information by email. Requests relating to information held by Google or Epic may also need to be submitted through their privacy channels.

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. Age-related requirements may differ by country. We do not knowingly ask players to submit their name, email address, telephone number or other direct contact details through the Game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps within our control to protect information handled by the Game.
